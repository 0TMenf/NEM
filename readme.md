## What is this plugin?

You are Notch, the creator of Minecraft. You heard that Gu Zhenxing, one of the founders of Mini World, has obtained the Mini World source code left behind by the "Jurassic Old Man" — based on the story of the "First Chinese Internet World War" between 2018 and 2022, the feud between the Minecraft and Mini World player communities. So you decided to sneak into Gu Zhenxing's house on a dark and windy night.

Only after arriving at Gu Zhenxing's house did you discover that it was filled with photos of you (Notch). It turns out that Gu Zhenxing is a yandere, and he is deeply in love with you. Just then, Gu Zhenxing quietly crept up behind you and knocked you out with a cooking pot.

When you wake up, you find yourself tied up in Gu Zhenxing's basement. It's dark, damp, and stiflingly hot. You accidentally overhear Gu Zhenxing's plan: "If I kill Notch, his body can stay with me forever."

You have 3 days (60 minutes) to escape Gu Zhenxing's house and call the police outside. Be careful not to get caught by Gu Zhenxing during your escape, or you'll be killed ahead of time.

## Gameplay Overview

NEM is a **multiplayer competitive** minigame that supports up to **24 players** (1–6 Gu Zhenxing + 2–18 Notch).

### Teams

| Team | Goal | Max Players |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notch | Escape the basement → Find keys → Burst through the front door → Hide in the abandoned house → Call the police | 18 |
| Gu Zhenxing | Kill (3 hits) or capture all Notch | 6 |

### Game Flow (Notch's Perspective)

1. **Wake up in the basement** — Find the **Basement Key**
2. **Go upstairs** — Find the **Toilet Key** → Open the toilet
3. **Inside the toilet** — Find the **Bedroom Key** → Open the bedroom
4. **Bedroom (DANGER!)** — Gu Zhenxing is sleeping. You **must sneak**, or you'll wake him up. Quietly grab the **Front Door Key**
5. **Burst through the front door** — The door makes a loud noise, waking Gu Zhenxing. You have **5 seconds** to get out
6. **Escape** — Reach the abandoned house within **30 seconds**
7. **Hide** — Stay **completely still for 30 seconds** inside the abandoned house. If you move even once, your whole body will glow and your location will be broadcast to all Gu Zhenxing players
8. **Call the police** — Reach the police station → **Victory!**
